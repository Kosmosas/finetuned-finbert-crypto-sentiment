\subsection{Selection of strategy hyperparameters}
\label{sec:strategy_hparam_selection_revised}

Strategy hyperparameters are tuned on the training period only, by chronological
cross-validation with Optuna. The training frame is divided into $K = 10$
contiguous folds (\texttt{KFold} without shuffling), so every fold is a
contiguous block of hours and no fold precedes its own training data.

The earlier procedure ran an independent Optuna study on each fold and then
averaged the per-fold winners, taking the arithmetic mean of the continuous
parameters and the rounded mean of the integer ones. That is not an optimisation
of any stated objective: the mean of a set of maximisers is not itself a
maximiser, it is undefined for integer and categorical parameters such as the
holding period, and the averaged configuration need not perform well on any
individual fold.

The procedure is therefore replaced by a single study over the same search space,
in which a candidate configuration $\theta$ is scored by its aggregate performance
across all folds,
%
\begin{equation}
\hat{\theta} \;=\; \arg\max_{\theta}\; \frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}
\mathrm{SR}\!\left(\theta; \mathcal{F}_k\right),
\label{eq:strategy_objective}
\end{equation}
%
where $\mathrm{SR}(\theta; \mathcal{F}_k)$ is the annualised Sharpe ratio obtained
by running the strategy with configuration $\theta$ over fold $\mathcal{F}_k$.
Each trial is thus evaluated on all ten folds, and the selected configuration is
the one that performs best overall rather than a summary of ten separate winners.
The computational cost is unchanged: the earlier scheme evaluated $K$ studies of
$N$ trials each, the revised one evaluates $N$ trials over $K$ folds.

Selecting a single configuration also makes fold-level dispersion reportable,
because the Sharpe ratio of \emph{the same} configuration can now be quoted on
each fold. This dispersion is substantial: the selected momentum configuration
ranges from $-1.44$ to $+3.42$ across the ten folds (standard deviation $1.45$),
and comparable spreads occur for the other strategies. Strategy-level point
estimates should therefore be read together with this variation rather than on
their own.

The change is material. Holding the forecasting model fixed and altering only the
selection rule, the momentum strategy on the price-and-volume feature set falls
from \$13{,}620 to \$10{,}749 over the uptrend evaluation period, a reduction of
roughly one fifth attributable entirely to the selection procedure.\footnote{The
Optuna study is executed with parallel workers, and the tree-structured Parzen
estimator is not deterministic under parallelism even for a fixed seed. Repeated
executions of identical code therefore return different configurations; the
reported figures are single draws, and differences of a few hundred dollars
between configurations are within this variation.}
